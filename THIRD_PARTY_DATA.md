# Third-party data and materials

## Licensing boundary

The MIT License in `LICENSE` covers only original research source code authored for this project. The CC BY 4.0 notice in `LICENSE-DATA-DOCS.md` covers only original derived data, original documentation, and original research-compendium materials for which the authors can grant a license.

Neither license applies to third-party source datasets, third-party libraries, copied third-party source material, or other externally owned materials. Those materials remain governed by their respective providers' terms.

## Source datasets

Raw third-party source datasets are not redistributed in this package. They must be obtained separately from their official providers. The documented source products include:

- Fire Event Data Suite (FEDS) Data Western US Archive
- USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP)
- LANDFIRE Remap fuel, canopy, and Existing Vegetation Type products
- NOAA/NCEP High-Resolution Rapid Refresh (HRRR) forecasts

`SOURCE_DATA.md` provides the exact known product identities, official sources or persistent identifiers, retained provenance evidence, and unresolved provenance limitations. Inclusion of derived analytical products does not transfer ownership of or alter the licensing terms for the underlying third-party source data.
