# Paper 1 Propagation Claim Boundaries

## Scope

These boundaries apply to the proposed FEDS-centered Brattain propagation experiment. They remain conditional until the repository's burn-severity blueprint and propagation direction are formally reconciled.

## Potentially allowed claims

Subject to successful locked validation and transparent limitations, Paper 1 may claim:

- incremental predictive information from a prespecified ecological information block during one observed wildfire;
- conditional next-interval incorporation into observed FEDS cumulative extent among t0-exposed, still-unburned units;
- comparative held-out performance within the Brattain Fire under whole-interval temporal validation;
- forward-chaining performance for later observed Brattain periods when that sensitivity is reported separately;
- geographic variation in held-out performance only as a sector-stratified within-fire diagnostic with adequate support;
- robustness or sensitivity to response grid, boundary uncertainty, early-phase inclusion, risk-set definition, and weather estimand;
- associations conditional on the observed fire state, measured predictors, FEDS observation process, and locked risk set;
- limitations arising from VIIRS/FEDS measurement support, spatial dependence, temporal dependence, suppression timing, and unmeasured processes.

Use `prediction` only with its validation scope. Use `forecast` only for the t0-information analysis based on HRRR forecasts initialized at or before t0. Use `retrospective conditional analysis` for realized t0-to-t1 weather.

## Forbidden claims

The proposed design cannot support claims of:

- causal ecological, fuel, terrain, weather, or suppression effects;
- ignition probability or why the fire started;
- unconditional landscape probability of burning;
- annual wildfire risk or general wildfire susceptibility;
- complete physical fire-spread reconstruction;
- exact 30 m or sub-VIIRS fire-edge prediction;
- exact spotting-event detection or attribution without independent spotting observations;
- a validated operational decision-support or deployment system;
- generalization to other fires, years, regions, fuel regimes, or management systems;
- universal wildfire prediction;
- independent observations equal to the number of candidate grid-cell rows;
- absence of suppression where retrospective line data are missing;
- a final perimeter as a legitimate source of negatives;
- future weather analyses, t1 geometry, future active-fire detections, or completed suppression lines as t0 forecast predictors;
- superiority selected after searching response grids, risk radii, intervals, outcomes, folds, or feature summaries by predictive score.

## Firebrand terminology

Until independent spotting observations and a source-transport-receiver parameter audit exist, the only permitted term is **physics-informed potential firebrand exposure**. It may be reported as exploratory sensitivity evidence, not as observed spotting probability, ember landing probability, or causal spotting mechanism.

## Generalization boundary

The scientific population represented by this experiment is the set of eligible observed Brattain intervals and t0-defined exposed locations under the FEDS measurement process. Whole-fire, cross-fire, regional, and prospective operational generalization require independent fires and belong to later research.

## Authorization boundary

These claim rules do not authorize model fitting. The propagation architecture remains conditional on formal project-authority reconciliation and completion of predictor, weather-value, common-support, and implementation audits.
